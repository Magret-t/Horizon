# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the craft value of timely video and music creation content for a creator who edits videos, produces music, and follows industry techniques.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** A technique, workflow, or tool update that significantly improves creative output or saves substantial time.
- **7-8: High value.** Detailed tutorials, professional workflows, notable software/gear updates with practical impact.
- **5-6: Useful.** Routine tips or minor updates with a practical takeaway.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Shallow coverage, repetitive tips, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer concrete, applicable content: specific steps, settings, gear names, and demonstrated results. Reward craft substance over hype.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
