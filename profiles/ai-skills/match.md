# AI practical skills profile

Use this profile for practical, skill-oriented content about artificial intelligence: new AI features and tools, prompting techniques, workflows, model capabilities, and hands-on tips that improve how people use AI in daily work.

Typical items include official announcements of new AI features, practical guides with concrete steps, workflow improvements, and comparisons of AI tools for specific tasks.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is research papers without practical use, finance-first AI coverage, or generic tech news without a practical AI angle.
