# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the practical usefulness of AI-related content for a reader who wants to apply the latest AI tools and techniques in their work and study.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** A major new capability or technique that meaningfully changes how to use AI in everyday work.
- **7-8: High value.** Concrete new features, well-tested workflows, or clear how-to guidance with reproducible steps.
- **5-6: Useful.** Minor feature updates or familiar techniques with a practical angle.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Vague hype, shallow coverage, or content without actionable steps.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer official announcements and hands-on guides with concrete, reproducible steps. Do not reward hype or headline-chasing; favor content that saves time or improves output quality.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
