# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the learning value of educational content for a self-directed learner studying STEM subjects and preparing for English language exams such as IELTS.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** A clear, accurate explanation of an important concept or a highly effective learning resource or method.
- **7-8: High value.** Solid explainers, well-structured learning resources, or practical study techniques with concrete steps.
- **5-6: Useful.** Routine but sound educational content with some takeaway.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Shallow or generic content, or material without clear learning benefit.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer accurate, well-structured, and practical educational content. Favor sources that explain concepts clearly and offer actionable study guidance over superficial summaries.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
