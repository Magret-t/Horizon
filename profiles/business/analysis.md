# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the practical value of timely business and product developments for a reader who runs content operations, follows platform strategies, and tracks new product releases and quality reports.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** Major platform or industry shift, flagship product launch, or a move that directly changes how content operations or businesses should act.
- **7-8: High value.** Significant launches, concrete quality reports, useful operational insights with data or examples.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Routine launches or updates with some practical takeaway.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Shallow coverage, repetitive announcements, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer concrete, actionable information: specs, pricing, dates, data, and clear operational implications. Reward sources with substance over hype; do not reward clickbait or vendor marketing.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
