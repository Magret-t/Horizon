# Clinical medicine learning profile

Use this profile for clinically oriented content useful for systematic medical learning: instructive case reports, specialty updates in internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics/gynecology, cardiology and electrocardiography, imaging and radiology, pathology, pharmacology, immunology, and biochemistry.

Typical items include case reports with clear teaching points, updated guidelines, drug and treatment updates, ECG patterns and interpretation, imaging findings, disease mechanisms, and practice-relevant clinical research.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is pure basic-science research without clinical relevance, general consumer health news, promotional material, or routine announcements without learning value.
