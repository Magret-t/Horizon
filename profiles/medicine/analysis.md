# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the learning value of timely clinical content for a medical student or clinician building a broad, up-to-date knowledge base across internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics, obstetrics/gynecology, cardiology and ECG, imaging, pathology, pharmacology, immunology, and biochemistry.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Essential.** Practice-changing guideline or trial, landmark case report with rare pathology or novel management, or a diagnostic pattern (ECG, imaging, labs) worth memorizing.
- **7-8: High value.** Well-presented instructive cases, updated guidelines or drug information, clear ECG/imaging teaching points, strong specialty updates.
- **5-6: Useful.** Routine but solid educational content, minor updates, or familiar topics presented with a useful angle.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Generic health content, shallow summaries, or material without concrete learning value.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Prioritize concrete educational value: clear clinical reasoning, differential diagnosis, management decisions, drug mechanisms, and ECG or imaging patterns. Prefer peer-reviewed and specialty-authoritative sources. Do not equate popularity with importance.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
