# Biomedical & AI research profile

Use this profile for timely developments in biomedical research, computational biology, bioinformatics, genomics, genetics, clinical medicine, drug discovery, medical artificial intelligence, and AI-for-science.

Typical items include significant new studies or preprints, validated computational methods or tools, major datasets, genome-wide association and multi-omics findings, clinical trials and practice-changing results, medical AI models and applications, and authoritative analysis tied to a recent result.

Do not use this profile for content whose primary value is general consumer news, finance-first coverage, step-by-step tutorials, routine product announcements, or promotional material without substantive scientific information.
