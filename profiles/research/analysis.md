# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the scientific importance of timely research findings for readers working in biomedical research, bioinformatics, genomics, clinical medicine, and AI-for-science.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Groundbreaking.** Landmark clinical trials or practice-changing results, paradigm-shifting methods, major model/dataset releases with wide impact, or discoveries that reshape a field.
- **7-8: High value.** Solid new methods with convincing validation, significant GWAS/multi-omics or clinical findings, influential preprints, and important tool releases worth prompt attention.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Incremental but sound contributions, useful methods or benchmarks, moderate field interest, or developments worth knowing but not urgent.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine updates, replications of known results, shallow coverage, or content dominated by promotion.
- **0-2: Noise.** Spam, off-topic material, trivial announcements, or purely promotional content.

# Evaluation guidance

Consider methodological rigor, novelty, validation quality and sample size, likely impact on the field, clinical relevance when applicable, source quality, and concrete supporting details. Favor peer-reviewed results and well-scrutinized preprints; do not equate popularity with scientific importance and do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
